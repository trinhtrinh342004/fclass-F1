## Muc tieu bai hoc

1. Nhan biet va dung duoc tu vung co ban ve Internet, tai khoan, mat khau, website, email va thao tac online.
2. Hieu va lam theo cau menh lenh khang dinh: `Open the website.`, `Click the link.`, `Type your username.`
3. Dung cau menh lenh phu dinh voi `Don't + V` de noi loi nhac an toan: `Don't share your password.`
4. Noi va viet quy trinh online ngan bang `First / Then / Next / Finally`.

### Bang cong thuc chinh

| # | Formula / Pattern | Meaning VI | Example EN | Example VI | Common mistake | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | V + object. | Menh lenh khang dinh de bao ai lam gi | Open the website. | Mo trang web. | Opens the website. | Menh lenh bat dau bang V nguyen mau, khong them -s |
| 2 | Don't + V + object. | Menh lenh phu dinh, canh bao khong lam gi | Don't share your password. | Dung chia se mat khau cua ban. | Don't shares your password. | Sau Don't dung V nguyen mau |
| 3 | Please + V + object. / V + object, please. | Menh lenh lich su | Please check your email. | Lam on kiem tra email cua ban. | Please to check your email. | Sau Please dung V nguyen mau |
| 4 | First, V. Then, V. Next, V. Finally, V. | Noi trinh tu thao tac | First, open the app. Then, type your password. | Dau tien, mo ung dung. Sau do, go mat khau. | Then typing your password. | Sau tu trinh tu van dung menh lenh V nguyen mau |
| 5 | Be careful with + noun. / Don't forget to + V. | Nhac nho an toan va viec can lam | Be careful with strange links. Don't forget to log out. | Can than voi link la. Dung quen dang xuat. | Don't forget log out. | Dung `Don't forget to + V` |

### Vi du luyen nhanh

- Click the blue button.
- Type your username carefully.
- Don't click strange links.
- Please send the message to your teacher.
- First, connect to Wi-Fi. Then, open the website. Finally, log out.

### Loi thuong gap

- Sai: `Don't clicks the link.` - Dung: `Don't click the link.`
- Sai: `Please to open the app.` - Dung: `Please open the app.`
- Sai: `First open website.` - Dung: `First, open the website.`
- Sai: `Share not your password.` - Dung: `Don't share your password.`
